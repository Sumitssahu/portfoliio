The Problem This Solved
⚠ The Problem

Leadership needed a strategic investment decision on which SaaS verticals to prioritise for 3 years — but had no reliable, current sizing data. Existing reports were 18 months old, vendor-biased, and didn't break down India's addressable market.

✓ What I Built

A bottom-up market sizing model across 5 verticals with AI-assisted source triangulation. Cut research time by 40%. Delivered a boardroom deck projecting growth from $158B → $317B that directly influenced a ₹2Cr+ budget allocation.

Executive Summary

Commissioned to size the global SaaS market for a strategic growth briefing to senior leadership. The analysis covered CRM, HR Tech, ERP, Collaboration, and Security — benchmarking market share, CAGR, vendor HHI scores, and pricing dynamics. AI-assisted workflows cut research time by 40%. The model projected a 14.6% blended CAGR from $158B to $317B by 2028, with Security SaaS and AI-embedded CRM as primary growth engines.

CAGR by Vertical 2023–2028
Market Size $B — 2020→2028 Projected
Key Findings
  • 01
    Security SaaS at 22% CAGR — outpacing CRM for the first time since 2019. Zero-trust enterprise adoption and mandatory cyber insurance are the twin demand drivers.
  • 02
    CRM dominates at 28% share but faces structural margin compression as AI-native mid-market players commoditise core features.
  • 03
    AI-embedded SaaS commands 15–18% price premium over feature-equivalent legacy tools — validated across 40+ vendor pricing pages.
  • 04
    HR Tech is the most fragmented vertical — top-5 players hold only 31% combined share (HHI: 0.12). Classic PE-backed rollup opportunity.
  • 05
    India sub-market at $14B growing 28% CAGR — materially underweighted in current global incumbent strategy.
Recommendation
Action Items

Prioritise Security SaaS and AI-native CRM for investment — highest CAGR, strongest pricing power. HR Tech warrants a consolidation play before organic consolidation resets valuations. Allocate dedicated India-market budget proportional to its 28% growth rate.

The Problem
⚠ Problem

Business needed to shortlist 2–3 fintech partners from 12 candidates. No structured framework — individual opinions driving the conversation. No visibility on regulatory risk or real user growth trajectories.

✓ Solution

A 2×2 competitive positioning matrix scoring 12 players across 8 dimensions. Surfaced 3 hidden gems and 4 regulatory risk zone players. Decision made within 2 weeks — 4 risky players eliminated before any due diligence spend.

Summary

Built a structured competitive intelligence framework mapping India's fintech landscape across payments, lending, and wealthtech. The 2×2 matrix (Growth Velocity vs Regulatory Safety) revealed 3 hidden gems in underserved tier-2/3 segments with >40% YoY growth, 4 players in active RBI scrutiny, and 2 dominant incumbents. The report directly eliminated 4 players and accelerated the partnership decision by 3 weeks.

2×2 Matrix — Growth Velocity vs Regulatory Safety
Player Feature Score (0–100)
Players by Fintech Segment
Key Findings
  • 01
    3 Hidden Gems — Slice, KreditBee, Jupiter. >40% YoY user growth at sub-₹500Cr valuations. Ideal low-cost, high-upside partnership targets.
  • 02
    4 Risk Zone players flagged — LazyPay, Uni, ZestMoney, ePayLater. Active RBI audit notices. Recommended exclusion for 12 months.
  • 03
    Market leaders (Razorpay, PhonePe) dominate payments but face margin compression as UPI commoditises transaction revenue.
  • 04
    Wealthtech is the highest white-space — only 3 scaled players for 200M+ investable-income users. SEBI sandbox creates 12–18 month tailwind.
Recommendation
Shortlist

Fast-track Slice and KreditBee — highest growth velocity, regulatory safety, API readiness. Add Zerodha for wealthtech. Avoid all 4 risk-zone players for 6–12 months.

The Problem
⚠ Problem

The BI team spent 3 full working days every month manually pulling from 4 siloed systems, cleaning in Excel, reconciling mismatches. Data was 4–5 days stale by the time leadership saw it. Errors were frequent. Single point of failure.

✓ Solution

A fully automated Power BI pipeline via Power Query with schema normalisation and a validation layer. Reporting time dropped from 3 days to 6 hours. Zero errors in 8+ months. Three Excel trackers replaced. Used daily by Sales, Finance, and Operations.

Summary

Designed and deployed a fully automated monthly reporting pipeline replacing a broken manual process. Power Query (M language) connects, cleans, and unifies 4 sources with automated schema mapping and anomaly detection. Since go-live the pipeline has run for 8+ consecutive months with zero structural failures, handling 3 source schema changes automatically. Management identifies revenue variances within 24 hours of month-end close vs 4–5 days previously.
