01
Market Research

Global SaaS Market Analysis

Excel PowerPoint Statista Gartner
Sector
Technology / SaaS
Type
Market Sizing + Trend Analysis
Deliverable
10-Page Research Report

Conducted a comprehensive bottom-up market sizing of the global SaaS industry. Analyzed 5-year growth trends across 5 verticals (CRM, ERP, HR Tech, Collaboration, Other) and identified high-potential segments for business expansion. Cross-validated findings against 3 independent external sources.

Key Findings

Global SaaS market reached $317B in 2024, growing at 18.4% CAGR — driven primarily by SMB adoption and AI-integrated platforms.

Asia-Pacific emerged as the fastest-growing region at 23% CAGR, with India and Southeast Asia as primary growth engines.

SaaS products with AI capabilities grew 2.8x faster than traditional peers — signaling a critical platform shift for vendors.

📈

Business Impact: Report used by leadership to shortlist 3 high-growth SaaS vendors for partnership discussions. Saved ~40 hours of manual research for the team.

02
Competitive Intelligence

Indian Fintech Competitive Landscape

Excel PowerPoint Public Filings
Sector
Fintech / BFSI
Type
Competitive Benchmarking
Deliverable
Competitive Matrix + Deck

Mapped the competitive landscape of India's fintech sector across payments, lending, and wealthtech. Built a 2×2 positioning matrix comparing 12 players on product breadth vs. customer acquisition cost. Analyzed funding rounds, partnerships, and regulatory exposure.

Key Findings

UPI-led payment players hold dominant market share but face margin compression — opportunity lies in adjacent financial services.

3 undercovered players in the SME lending space showed strong unit economics with significantly lower CAC than incumbents.

Regulatory risk concentration identified in 4 players — flagged as key watchlist items for risk monitoring.

🎯

Business Impact: Competitive matrix adopted as a standard template by the team. Directly informed a vendor shortlisting decision within 2 weeks of delivery.
