Problem & Solution
⚠ Problem

Leadership had no current market data to decide which SaaS verticals to invest in. Reports were 18 months old, vendor-biased, no India breakdown. No unified model existed.

✓ What I Built

Bottom-up sizing model across 5 verticals via AI-assisted triangulation (Gartner, Statista, IBISWorld). Cut research time by 40%. Delivered boardroom deck in 10 days.

Model projected a 14.6% blended CAGR from $158B to $317B by 2028, with Security SaaS and AI-embedded CRM as primary growth engines. Report directly shaped a multi-crore budget decision within 2 weeks of delivery.

Key Findings
01
Security SaaS at 22% CAGR — outpacing CRM since 2019. Zero-trust adoption and mandatory cyber insurance are the twin demand drivers.
02
CRM dominates at 28% market share but faces margin compression. AI-native players commoditise core features. HHI dropping 0.28 → 0.22 signals rapid fragmentation.

Prioritise Security SaaS and AI-native CRM. HR Tech warrants a consolidation play. Allocate dedicated India budget proportional to its 28% CAGR.

Problem & Solution
⚠ Problem

Business needed to shortlist 2–3 fintech partners from 12 candidates. No structured framework — opinions driving decisions with zero visibility on regulatory risk.

✓ What I Built

A 2×2 positioning matrix scoring all 12 players across 8 dimensions: product breadth, compliance posture, funding stage, user growth, API readiness and more.

The 2×2 (Growth Velocity vs Regulatory Safety) revealed 3 hidden gems with >40% YoY growth, 4 players in active RBI scrutiny, and 2 dominant incumbents. Report eliminated 4 players before any due diligence spend, accelerating the decision by 3 weeks.

Positioning Matrix & Scores
2×2 Matrix — Growth Velocity vs Regulatory Safety
Composite Score by Player (0–100)
Key Findings
01
3 Hidden Gems identified — Slice, KreditBee, Jupiter. All show >40% YoY user growth at sub-₹500Cr valuations.
02
4 Risk Zone players flagged — LazyPay, Uni, ZestMoney, ePayLater. All carry active RBI Show Cause notices. Recommended exclusion for 12+ months.
03
Wealthtech highest white-space — only 3 scaled players for 200M+ investable-income users.
04
Market leaders face margin compression as UPI commoditises transaction revenue. Moat shifting to data monetisation and embedded credit products.
Shortlist Decision

Fast-track Slice and KreditBee. Add Zerodha for wealthtech co-distribution. Avoid all 4 risk-zone players for minimum 12 months.

Day 1

12-player universe finalised. 8 scoring dimensions agreed. Axes: Growth Velocity vs Regulatory Safety.
Day 2–5
Data Collection & Scoring
Scraped Crunchbase, RBI filings, MCA data, App Store ratings, LinkedIn headcount. Weighted scoring in Excel.
Day 6–8
Matrix Build & Validation
2×2 plotted. Classifications validated against 3 independent sources each. Peer-reviewed before submission.
Day 9–10
Deck Delivery & Decision
10-slide deck delivered. Partnership decision finalised same week. Saved 3+ weeks of due diligence.
