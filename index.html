Indian Fintech Competitive Landscape

ExcelPowerPointTrelloQualitative DataProduct Analysis
Sector
Fintech / BFSI
Type
Competitive + Product Benchmarking
Deliverable
Competitive Matrix + Deck

Mapped the competitive landscape of India's fintech sector across payments, lending, and wealthtech. Built a 2×2 product positioning matrix comparing 12 players on feature breadth vs. customer acquisition cost. Used Trello to manage qualitative research data across multiple sources, enabling structured analysis of funding rounds and regulatory exposure.

Key Findings

UPI-led players hold dominant market share but face margin compression — opportunity lies in adjacent financial services.

3 undercovered players in SME lending showed strong unit economics with significantly lower CAC than incumbents.

Regulatory risk concentration identified in 4 players — flagged as key watchlist items for risk monitoring.

🎯

Business Impact: Competitive matrix adopted as a standard template by the team. Directly informed a vendor shortlisting decision within 2 weeks of delivery.
