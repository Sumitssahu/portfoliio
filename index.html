⚠ The Problem

Leadership needed a strategic investment decision on which SaaS verticals to prioritise for 3 years — but had no reliable current sizing data. Existing reports were 18 months old, vendor-biased, and did not break down India's addressable market. The team was spending weeks collecting fragmented data with no unified model or framework.

✓ What I Built

A bottom-up market sizing model across 5 verticals with AI-assisted source triangulation (Gartner, Statista, IBISWorld). Cut research time by 40%. Delivered a boardroom deck projecting growth from $158B → $317B with per-vertical CAGR, HHI scores, and a white-space opportunity map that directly influenced a ₹2Cr+ budget allocation.

Executive Summary

Commissioned to size the global SaaS market for a strategic growth briefing to senior leadership. The analysis covered CRM, HR Tech, ERP, Collaboration, and Security verticals — benchmarking market share, CAGR, vendor concentration, and pricing dynamics. AI-assisted prompt workflows cut research time by 40% while improving triangulation quality across three primary data sources. The final model projected a 14.6% blended CAGR from $158B to $317B by 2028, with Security SaaS and AI-embedded CRM as primary growth engines. The report directly shaped a multi-crore budget allocation decision within two weeks of delivery.

Key Findings
  • 01
    Security SaaS at 22% CAGR — outpacing CRM for the first time since 2019. Zero-trust enterprise adoption and mandatory cyber insurance requirements post-2023 regulations are the twin demand drivers.
  • 02
    CRM dominates at 28% share but faces structural margin compression. AI-native mid-market players are commoditising core features; HHI concentration is dropping from 0.28 to a projected 0.22 within 3 years.

    AI-embedded SaaS commands 15–18% price premium over feature-equivalent legacy tools — validated across 40+ vendor pricing pages. Buyers are willing to pay for automation that quantifiably reduces FTE headcount.
  • 04
    HR Tech is the most fragmented vertical — top-5 players hold only 31% combined share (HHI: 0.12). Classic PE-backed rollup opportunity; consolidation could generate 3–4× EBITDA multiples within 5 years.
  • 05
    India sub-market at $14B growing 28% CAGR — materially underweighted in current global incumbent strategy. Tier-2 city adoption and government cloud mandates represent a structural demand shift that global vendors are 12–18 months slow to price in.
Strategic Recommendation
Action Items

Prioritise Security SaaS and AI-native CRM for investment — highest CAGR, strongest pricing power, most defensible moats. HR Tech warrants a consolidation play; move before organic consolidation resets valuations upward over the next 18 months. Allocate dedicated India-market budget proportional to its 28% growth rate vs the 14.6% blended global rate.

The Problem This Solved
⚠ The Problem

The business needed to shortlist 2–3 fintech partners from 12 candidates for a distribution tie-up. There was no structured framework — individual opinions were driving the conversation. No visibility on regulatory risk, actual user growth trajectories, or comparative moat strength. A wrong pick could mean regulatory exposure or a partnership with a player about to hit a compliance wall.

✓ What I Built

A 2×2 competitive positioning matrix scoring 12 players across 8 dimensions — product breadth, compliance posture, funding trajectory, user growth, market share, revenue defensibility, API readiness, and UX maturity. Surfaced 3 hidden gems, 2 market leaders, and 4 regulatory risk zone players. The partnership decision was made within 2 weeks, with 4 risky players de-selected before any due diligence spend.

Executive Summary

Built a structured competitive intelligence framework mapping India's fintech landscape across payments, lending, and wealthtech. The 2×2 matrix plotted players on Growth Velocity vs Regulatory Safety axes, revealing a clear stratification. Three hidden gems in underserved tier-2/3 lending segments with >40% YoY user growth. Four players in active RBI regulatory scrutiny. Two dominant incumbents with API-ready infrastructure. The report directly eliminated 4 players from the shortlist before any due diligence spend and accelerated the partnership decision by an estimated 3 weeks. Slice and KreditBee were recommended as primary targets.

The Problem This Solved
⚠ The Problem

The BI team spent 3 full working days every month manually pulling data from 4 siloed systems — CRM, finance, marketing, and ops. Data was cleaned in Excel, reconciled manually, and built into a static report. By the time leadership saw the numbers they were 4–5 days stale. Errors were frequent. The entire process was a single point of failure tied to one person with no documentation.

✓ What I Built

A fully automated Power BI pipeline consolidating all 4 sources via Power Query with schema normalisation and an automated data validation layer. Live MoM + YoY variance tracking across all KPIs. Reporting time dropped from 3 days to 6 hours. Zero manual errors in 8+ months. Three separate Excel trackers replaced. Now used daily by Sales, Finance, and Operations — with no manual intervention since go-live.

Executive Summary

Designed and deployed a fully automated monthly reporting pipeline replacing a broken manual process consuming 3 days of analyst time per month. The solution used Power Query (M language) to connect, clean, and unify 4 data sources with automated schema mapping and anomaly detection. The Power BI front-end delivers interactive KPI dashboards with live MoM and YoY variance for Revenue, Leads, Conversion, and Churn. Since go-live the pipeline has run for 8+ consecutive months with zero structural failures, handling 3 source schema changes automatically. Management can now identify revenue variances within 24 hours of month-end close vs 4–5 days previously — enabling faster corrective action in every monthly cycle.
